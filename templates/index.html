Old Millstone Forge

The Old Millstone Forge is home to what was the longest operating blacksmith shop in America. It served the area as an active blacksmith’s shop until the death of its last blacksmith Edward H. Wyckoff, in 1959, who served a remarkable 64 years. The building was restored by area residents in the 1960s and is currently operating as a blacksmith shop and living history museum by the Millstone Forge Association.
